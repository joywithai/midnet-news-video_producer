# MidNet News logo asset

`midnet-news-logo-hd.png` is the supplied `source-original.jpg` with only the connected white exterior removed. The supplied artwork's design, text, shape, colors and proportions were preserved; the 600×600 source was enlarged to 1200×1200 with Lanczos resampling for an HD transparent PNG. The original upload is retained unchanged as `source-original.jpg`.

Use the PNG as the video watermark and thumbnail mark. Do not edit/recolor/recreate it. Its transparent alpha channel was checked, including compositing on white. Video sizing may scale it proportionally while the source asset remains untouched.
