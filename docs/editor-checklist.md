# MidNet News pre-publication review checklist

## Brand and layout
- [ ] Verify logo file is the supplied original transparent PNG; no altered shape, color, text or proportions.
- [ ] Check top-corner placement, consistent size/opacity and safe margin; ensure it never masks captions, headlines or the subject.
- [ ] Confirm licensed commercial fonts and consistent headline/body/caption hierarchy.
- [ ] Inspect 16:9 framing, no stretching, cropping errors, clipping or off-screen text.

## Story and assets
- [ ] Play the entire audio and compare every caption, proper noun, number, punctuation and subtitle timestamp.
- [ ] Verify every person, place, logo, document and event against a trusted source; do not rely on filename or AI inference.
- [ ] Check source URL, license, attribution and date/context for every asset; label archive or illustrative media.
- [ ] Confirm visual changes occur at relevant narration beats, no unjustified repetition, misleading juxtaposition or unsupported map/stat.
- [ ] Confirm each thumbnail represents the same story as the video and title; no fabricated real-world scene.

## Technical
- [ ] Watch full-resolution and at mobile preview size; check transitions, black/frozen frames and logo/caption safe areas.
- [ ] Listen on headphones and speakers; check sync, voice consistency, clipping, loudness and silences.
- [ ] Confirm 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, complete decode and file duration.
- [ ] Confirm three distinct 1280×720 thumbnails and correct upload title/description.
- [ ] Verify sourceRecord and productionReport are included in delivery.

Record reviewer and attestations in `input/qc-approval.json` only after completing checks. Any failed item keeps the package in `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
