# YouTube Thumbnail Design Skill Contract

**Purpose:** create three factually faithful and visually distinct 1280×720 thumbnail concepts.

- **Inputs:** three thumbnail concepts, three titles, verified assets, original transparent logo, licensed font files, MidNet theme.
- **Process:** compose three different layouts/focal points (not mere color swaps); concise 2–5-word thumbnail copy; maintain strong contrast and mobile-size legibility; safe zones; no unverified imagery or misleading emotional juxtaposition.
- **Outputs:** `newsThumbnail1.png`, `newsThumbnail2.png`, `newsThumbnail3.png` only after QC; drafts named `draftThumbnailN.png` before that.
- **Validation:** dimensions/aspect ratio, distinct composition, 120-pixel preview legibility, logo intact, title/video factual consistency, rights and source record.
- **Failure:** unsuitable assets, too much copy or unsupported claim → request edit; do not synthesize a real-event photo.

**v0.1 status:** Pillow makes three distinct layout treatments at 1280×720 with optional approved local visuals and unchanged logo overlay. Mobile readability, factual accuracy and rights still require human review; no AI image generation is used.
